ABOUT THIS DATA

Clarity behind the numbers.

This project uses a historical snapshot of NASDAQ Daily Stock Prices on Kaggle, published by Steven Van Ingelgem (svaningelgem). The original upstream data owners and source sites are not identified in the dataset description. Prices, currency, and corporate-action adjustments have not been independently verified. Returns are price changes, not total returns or investment advice.
