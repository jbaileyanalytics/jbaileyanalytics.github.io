04 / METHODOLOGY

Know what you’re looking at.

Efficiency is calculated using total yards divided by total targets, carries, or pass attempts. Catch rate is receptions divided by targets; completion rate is completions divided by attempts. Player eligibility is based on opportunities within the selected filters.

Weekly averages use observed records in the selected scope; missing weeks are not treated as zero. Data includes regular-season quarterback, running back, wide receiver, and tight end player-week records. The 2026 snapshot is in progress and may be incomplete or revised. These are box-score statistics, not tracking data or live scores.
