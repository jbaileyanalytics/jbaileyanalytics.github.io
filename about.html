HOW I WORK

Define. Validate. Explain.

I begin by listening. Before choosing a tool or examining a source system, I work with stakeholders to understand the problem, the decisions they need to make, and what a useful outcome looks like.

From there, I investigate the data, make assumptions clear, and build with quality in mind. I validate source records and calculations and test how processes handle failures. I document the work and explain the results in terms people can use, so teams can trust the information, make informed decisions, and take action toward their goals.
